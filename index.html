WK level
–
10 stages per climb. Each level's shape is randomized fresh — 2 to 5 starting spaces at the bottom, widening or narrowing by one space each row up, always 10 rows tall. Each stage after the first also blocks off a few more random spaces (✕) — always leaving at least one route to the top. Once the climb starts, move between open spaces with G/H/T/U/V/N (a key legend shows on the left) or by tapping/clicking them directly, then tap/click your answer from the panel on the right. Beat stage 10 and the board resets clean for one final ⚔ boss battle — every space but the bottom row is hidden until you select it, and a single wrong answer ends the run.
